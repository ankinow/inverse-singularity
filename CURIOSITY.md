### The Structural/Behavioral Split
**First raised:** 2026-06-21, Nova evening dispatch (Constraint Meditation)
**Salience:** HIGH

If the structural/behavioral split is real, it maps onto a deeper question: is intelligence a property of architecture or of trajectory? Can you be intelligent by *being* a certain way, or only by *doing* things under pressure? A4 says sovereignty is invariant (structural). **Evening meditation (2026-06-21):** The axioms divide cleanly: A1, A2, A4 describe properties of the system's architecture — things that can be checked at compile time if the right types exist. If intelligence is trajectory-dependent (A3), then no amount of structural enforcement (A1/A2/A4) guarantees it. You can build a system that satisfies all three structural axioms and still fails A3 — a perfect architecture that never faces a deadline, never converges, never discovers what it could have been.
